how it works

01
start with what you share

A neighborhood. An interest. A life stage. A question. A favorite way to spend an evening. hodgepodge begins with the things that already connect people.

02
bring people together

Create a gathering around what matters to you. Build the guest list yourself, invite the people you know, or open the invitation to others with something in common.

03
let connection take shape

The gathering is only the beginning. hodgepodge creates more opportunities for people to meet, return, contribute, and become part of each other's lives, however that connection grows.

more life in common

hodgepodge helps turn the things people share into reasons to find one another, make time, and become part of each other's lives.

Not just new followers. More familiar faces, more shared stories, and more of a life that happens together.
